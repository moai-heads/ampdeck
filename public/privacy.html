AMPDECK / PRIVACY NOTICE

Your music stays yours.

Last updated: October 2, 2026. Ampdeck is a static, non-commercial browser prototype. It has no Ampdeck account system, analytics, advertising, or token backend.

Spotify login and playback

If you choose Spotify, Ampdeck uses Spotify's official Web Playback SDK and Web API. Login is handled by Spotify through OAuth Authorization Code with PKCE. Ampdeck does not ask for or receive your Spotify password. The app's public Client ID is saved in browser local storage; OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in the current tab's session storage and cleared when you disconnect. Do not use this prototype on a shared or untrusted browser session.

Your browser sends authorization, search, and playback requests directly to Spotify. Spotify serves the protected audio to its Web Playback SDK; Ampdeck does not receive, record, download, or analyze Spotify audio. Spotify processes your account and playback data under its own privacy policy and terms. Spotify Premium is required for Web Playback SDK streaming.

Spotify track metadata and album art are displayed with Spotify attribution and links to the relevant track. The classic visualizer receives no Spotify audio signal. Local and Spotify playback are kept separate and are never mixed by Ampdeck.

Local audio and skins

Audio files and classic skin archives you select are read by your browser and are not uploaded by Ampdeck. Imported file blobs are kept for the current page session. Your selected color palette and Spotify Client ID are saved in local storage; OAuth tokens remain in session storage.

Third parties

If you choose to connect Spotify, the page loads Spotify's Web Playback SDK and sends OAuth, search, and playback requests directly to Spotify. Fonts load from Google Fonts. No third-party analytics or advertising trackers are included. Spotify and Google may process network data according to their own policies when their resources are requested.
