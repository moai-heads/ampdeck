AMPDECK / PRIVACY NOTICE

Your music stays yours.

Last updated: October 2, 2026. Ampdeck is a static, browser-only music player. It has no Ampdeck backend, analytics, advertising, or account system.

Spotify

Ampdeck does not embed Spotify or access Spotify APIs. If you paste a supported Spotify link and choose Open, Ampdeck opens its canonical Spotify URL in a separate tab. Spotify handles any login, playback, cookies, account access, and personal data under its own terms and privacy policy. Ampdeck does not receive your Spotify password, API token, account profile, or playback history.

After you leave Ampdeck, Spotify may set cookies and process data in its own site or app. Review Spotify's privacy policy and manage cookies there or in your browser. Spotify determines playback availability and any free-trial eligibility; Ampdeck does not grant trials.

Local audio and skins

Audio files and classic skin archives you choose are read locally by your browser and are not uploaded by Ampdeck. They remain in use only for this page session. Your pasted Spotify link is held only in the current page session. The selected color palette is saved in local storage.

Third parties

Spotify links open Spotify services and are subject to Spotify's terms and privacy policy. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts. No Spotify content or Spotify widget is loaded into Ampdeck, and no third-party analytics or tracking is included.

Questions

Ampdeck is an open-source prototype. See the user terms and source repository to inspect its behavior.
